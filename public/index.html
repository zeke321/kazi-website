About KAZI

Our mission & model

KAZI Startfunding is a non-profit association founded in Switzerland. We give interest-free loans to local entrepreneurs and groups whose sustainable projects cannot get bank financing.

Loans are indexed in local currency, so borrowers never carry the exchange-rate risk. Local teams choose the projects, follow them closely and mentor the entrepreneurs. When a loan is repaid, the money is lent again to someone new.

What every project must show

  • 📈Economic viability
  • 🤝A positive social impact
  • 🌱Respect for the environment
Our story

From an idea to three local teams

KAZI's story starts with the experience of our president, Paul-Camille Genton, first in Cameroon and then in Ifakara, Tanzania. The first loans were granted in Ifakara in 2019. In December 2022, young Chadians founded FEDJIN KAZI Chad to bring the same model to south-western Chad.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for a KAZI loan?

Entrepreneurs and groups in the areas where our local teams work (Ifakara in Tanzania, and south-western Chad) with a sustainable project that banks won't finance. The project must be economically viable and have a positive social and environmental impact. See how to apply.

Why are the loans interest-free?

Our goal is not profit but a fair start. Without interest, entrepreneurs keep more of what their business earns and can invest it in growing.

Why are loans indexed in local currency?

Borrowers receive and repay their loan in Tanzanian shillings or CFA francs. KAZI absorbs the exchange-rate risk, so a fall in the local currency never increases what an entrepreneur owes.

Why a loan and not a donation?

A repaid loan funds the next entrepreneur, so each franc helps several projects over time. Our local teams select every borrower carefully, and a good repayment record opens the door to a second loan.

How is my donation used?

Donations go into the loan fund. A small share covers our very low running costs, and our Swiss team are all volunteers. Each year's activity report shows how the money was used. See the reports.

How do I become a member or mentor?

Write to us at communication@kazi-funding.org and tell us how you would like to help. We will explain the options and next steps. More about getting involved.
